<div class="mdjTexte">
  «&nbsp;Je préfère aux biens<br /> dont s’enivre L’orgueil du soldat ou du roi,<br /> L’ombre que tu fais sur mon livre<br /> Quand ton front se penche sur moi.&nbsp;»
</div>

<div class="mdjAuteur">
  Victor Hugo «Les contemplations»
</div>

<span style="color: #777777; font-family: Arial;">Pour la saint Valentin, ce poème de Victor Hugo&nbsp;:<br /> </span>

<p style="margin-left: 28pt;">
  <span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Aimons toujours ! Aimons encore ! </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Quand l&rsquo;amour s&rsquo;en va, l&rsquo;espoir fuit. </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">L&rsquo;amour, c&rsquo;est le cri de l&rsquo;aurore, </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">L&rsquo;amour c&rsquo;est l&rsquo;hymne de la nuit.</span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span>
</p>

<p style="margin-left: 28pt;">
  <span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Ce que le flot dit aux rivages, </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Ce que le vent dit aux vieux monts, </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Ce que l&rsquo;astre dit aux nuages, </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">C&rsquo;est le mot ineffable : Aimons !</span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span>
</p>

<p style="margin-left: 28pt;">
  <span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">L&rsquo;amour fait songer, vivre et croire. </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Il a pour réchauffer le cœur, </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Un rayon de plus que la gloire, </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Et ce rayon c&rsquo;est le bonheur !</span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span>
</p>

<p style="margin-left: 28pt;">
  <span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Aime ! qu&rsquo;on les loue ou les blâme, </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Toujours les grands cœurs aimeront : </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Joins cette jeunesse de l&rsquo;âme </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">A la jeunesse de ton front !</span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span>
</p>

<p style="margin-left: 28pt;">
  <span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Aime, afin de charmer tes heures ! </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Afin qu&rsquo;on voie en tes beaux yeux </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Des voluptés intérieures </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Le sourire mystérieux !</span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span>
</p>

<p style="margin-left: 28pt;">
  <span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Aimons-nous toujours davantage ! </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Unissons-nous mieux chaque jour. </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Les arbres croissent en feuillage ; </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000; font-family: Arial;">Que notre âme croisse en amour !<br /> </span>
</p>

<p style="margin-left: 28pt;">
  <span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Soyons le miroir et l&rsquo;image ! </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Soyons la fleur et le parfum ! </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Les amants, qui, seuls sous l&rsquo;ombrage, </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Se sentent deux et ne sont qu&rsquo;un ! </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span>
</p>

<p style="margin-left: 28pt;">
  <span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">[..].</span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span>
</p>

<p style="margin-left: 28pt;">
  <span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Moi qui ne cherche dans ce monde </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Que la seule réalité, </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Moi qui laisse fuir comme l&rsquo;onde </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Tout ce qui n&rsquo;est que vanité,</span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span>
</p>

<p style="margin-left: 28pt;">
  <span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">Je préfère aux biens dont s&rsquo;enivre </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">L&rsquo;orgueil du soldat ou du roi, </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000;"><span style="font-family: Arial;">L&rsquo;ombre que tu fais sur mon livre </span><span style="font-family: Times New Roman;"><br /> </span></span><span style="color: #c00000; font-family: Arial;">Quand ton front se penche sur moi.<br /> </span>
</p>

[<span style="font-family: Arial;"><span style="color: #0000ff;"><Si vous voulez lire le poème en intégralité></span></span>][1]<span style="color: #777777; font-family: Arial;"><br /> </span>

<span style="color: #777777; font-family: Arial;">Pour Annie et moi, la Saint Valentin ne se fête pas le 14 février, mais le 15.<br /> </span><span style="color: #777777; font-family: Arial;">Le 15 février, il y a 30 ans, nous nous sommes rencontrés à Paris et quelques mois plus tard, nous ne nous quittions plus.<br /> </span>

<span style="color: #777777; font-family: Arial;">Il n&rsquo;y aura pas de mot du jour le 15 et le 16 février.<br /> </span>
